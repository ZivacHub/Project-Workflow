# Product: Weather note
Status: CONFIRMED by Sam 2026-01-05.

## The goal, in the owner's own words
> "I never know what to wear in the morning. I want one page where I type the temperature and it tells me." (Sam, 2026-01-05)

## Who it's for and what problem it solves
- Sam, every morning. Today Sam opens a weather app, reads too much, and still guesses.

## Non-negotiables
- One page, works offline, no account, no tracking.

## What success looks like
- Typing a temperature gives an answer in under a second, and Sam uses it for a week.

## Not doing (on purpose)
- Forecasts, locations, rain warnings. Maybe later.
